Privacy

Solution Studio keeps this simple: this website collects as little as possible.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, general location, and device type. No personally identifying profile is built or sold. You can block analytics with standard browser tools and the site works fine.

Email

If you email hello@solutionstud.io, the message is used only to respond to you. Addresses are never sold or added to lists without consent.

Contact forms

When you submit a form on this site, the details you provide are used only to follow up on your inquiry.

Cookies

Only the cookies Google Analytics sets for measurement. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set by this site.
